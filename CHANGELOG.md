## 1.1.0

### Espanol

El modpack se actualizo a la version 1.1.0. Se instala solo la proxima vez que abras el juego desde Prism.

**Rendimiento**
- The Broken Script ahora viene dividido en dos archivos (codigo y recursos) y carga mas liviano.
- TBS Additions: arreglada la cola de tareas que se recorria entera en cada tick. Era lo que mas lag le metia al servidor.
- TBS Blackout: los rayos que miran a donde apuntas ya no cargan ni generan chunks lejanos (eran congelones de casi medio segundo).
- Resultado: el servidor tarda cerca de un 40% menos por tick.

**Dreamshift**
- Traducido al espanol: menu, subtitulos, carteles y libros.
- Los dreamspaces que quedan vacios se descargan solos. Antes se quedaban cargados y el lag subia cada vez que visitabas uno.
- Arreglado un bucle entre Journey's Station y Angelic Station que daba tirones.

**Texturas y sonidos**
- Todo en un solo resourcepack, **TBS Nostalgia**: Nostalgia+, sonidos clasicos, la traduccion de TBS y los aldeanos con su skin de 1.8.
- El modo supervivencia de TBS ahora se llama "Andate".
- Tambien en espanol de Espana.

**Otros**
- The Obsessed ya no muestra el mensaje de bienvenida.
- Essential mas limpio: sin emotes, cosmeticos, barra de acciones rapidas ni avisos de Discord. Sigue sirviendo para hostear y unirse a mundos.
- Se saco spark (era solo para medir el rendimiento).
- Se saco Cinematic Interaction.

**Importante:** desde la 1.1.1, TBS Nostalgia se activa solo.

### English

The modpack was updated to version 1.1.0. It installs on its own the next time you launch the game from Prism.

**Performance**
- The Broken Script now comes split into two files (code and assets) and loads lighter.
- TBS Additions: fixed the task queue that was walked in full every tick. It was the biggest source of server lag.
- TBS Blackout: the look-at rays no longer load or generate far-away chunks (they caused freezes of almost half a second).
- Result: the server takes about 40% less time per tick.

**Dreamshift**
- Translated to Spanish: menu, subtitles, signs and books.
- Empty dreamspaces now unload on their own. Before, they stayed loaded and lag grew with every one you visited.
- Fixed a loop between Journey's Station and Angelic Station that caused stutters.

**Textures and sounds**
- Everything in a single resourcepack, **TBS Nostalgia**: Nostalgia+, classic sounds, the TBS translation and villagers with their 1.8 skin.
- The TBS survival game mode is now called "Andate".
- Also available in Spain Spanish.

**Other**
- The Obsessed no longer shows its welcome message.
- Cleaner Essential: no emotes, cosmetics, quick action bar or Discord notices. It still works for hosting and joining worlds.
- Removed spark (it was only for performance testing).
- Removed Cinematic Interaction.

**Important:** since 1.1.1, TBS Nostalgia turns on by itself.
